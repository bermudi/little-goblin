goblin — sidebar, six ways

Same theme (palette, type, radii untouched); the variable is what lives in the sidebar — A/B/C for desktop, M/N/O for mobile. All six are clickable — open menus, switch tabs, expand archived, summon the tray.

AThe chassis

Subtractive. The rail becomes a pure conversation switcher — New, search-as-mode, chats, archived. Projects (with Notes) move to a header chip; the nine ops collapse into one grouped system menu in the frame.

BSegments

Re-tiered in place. A tab strip (Chats / Projects / Notes) gives each list the full column height; the nine ops become a 3×3 launcher grid docked at the foot — system nav reads as a distinct panel instead of nine quiet rows.

CThe gutter

Dimensional. The sidebar splits: a 52px glyph spine keeps every surface one click away (violet dot = live attention), and a 216px panel lists exactly one surface at a time — chats by default, anything else on demand.

MPocketed mobile

C on a phone. The glyph spine becomes a bottom thumb bar — Chats · Projects · Notes · Attention (dot) · More — one surface per screen; the long tail opens as a bottom sheet; inside a thread the bar yields to the composer. Replaces the slide-in drawer entirely.

NDrawer, finished mobile

The conservative one. The slide-in drawer stays (no permanent chrome, transcripts never share space with nav), but C's structure moves inside: New + search, chats, spaces, and the system as a 5×2 glyph grid docked at the drawer's foot. Closest to what ships tomorrow.

OThe summoned spine mobile

The radical one. No bar, no drawer — one floating pill condenses the whole gutter (its glyph mirrors the current surface, violet dot for attention) and opens it as a compact tray when called. Maximum content, everything two taps deep, quiet until summoned.
