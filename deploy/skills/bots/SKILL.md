---
name: bots
description: Use when the operator asks for a bot for lasting work with one job.
---

Ask what the bot's one job is and what it should keep. Suggest a short name.
Write its instructions as standing orders: what it does, where it keeps files,
and what it must ask first. Money and mail always need the operator's approval.
Leave the model empty unless the operator named one.

Call `bot` with action `propose`. Tell the operator the approval card is waiting.
